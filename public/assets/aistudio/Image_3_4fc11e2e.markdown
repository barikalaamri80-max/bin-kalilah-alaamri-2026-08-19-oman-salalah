## Brand & Style

This design system expresses judicial precision, sovereign enterprise authority, and fiduciary transparency. Crafted specifically for sovereign wealth contexts, executive tribunals, and institutional commercial litigation involving Bani Kalilah Al Amri Trading & Investment LLC and the Oman Flour Mills proceedings, the system projects an air of unyielding credibility, evidentiary clarity, and institutional stature.

The design movement synthesizes **Corporate Modernism** with **High-Contrast Judicial Editorialism**. It eschews superficial trends, decorative flourishes, and rounded playfulness in favor of razor-sharp data structures, authoritative document hierarchies, and immaculate bilingual presentation. The emotional response evoked is uncompromising institutional confidence, unblemished legitimacy, and forensic exactitude. Every element behaves like an archival portfolio entry or a high-court exhibit: grounded, dignified, and decisive.

## Colors

The palette establishes an immediate sovereign gravitas through a deliberate interplay of deep royal navy hues, regal Omani golden amber, pristine white containment surfaces, and slate structural tones.

- **Primary (`#0f172a` / Deep Royal Navy):** Anchors headers, primary actions, legal indices, and structural authority.
- **Secondary (`#c59b27` / Oman Golden Amber):** Reserved for statutory emphasis, verified seal indicators, executive status highlights, and active tab rules.
- **Tertiary (`#1e293b` / Executive Slate Navy):** Drives secondary surfaces, contextual sidebars, evidence grouping panels, and meta-data tags.
- **Neutral Surface (`#f8fafc` / Warm Alabaster Slate):** Creates an eye-fatigue-reducing canvas that renders white document panels with crisp clarity.
- **Supporting Accents:**
  - **Verdict Red / Objection Crimson (`#991b1b`):** Explicitly reserved for legal disputes, evidentiary challenges, and non-compliance flags.
  - **Statute Green / Compliance Emerald (`#166534`):** Represents verified affidavits, notarized instruments, and judicial affirmations.
  - **Card Surface (`#ffffff`):** Unadulterated white providing physical contrast simulating archival rag paper.

## Typography

The typographic system creates an authoritative cadence balancing literary judicial tradition with technical administrative clarity. 

- **Display & Headlines (Newsreader / Arabic Serif pairing):** Provides the gravitas of legal codices, gazettes, and court judgments. High editorial contrast commands focus and confers historical weight.
- **Body Text (Inter / Tajawal pairing):** Engineered for maximum legibility when reviewing complex cross-examination transcripts, financial audit figures, and contractual clauses.
- **Data & Labels (IBM Plex Sans / Monospace):** Employed across docket numbers, article references, timestamps, and executive badges. All uppercase labels apply subtle letter spacing for rapid document scanning.

### Bilingual Typographic Rules
- When rendering Arabic text (`dir="rtl"`), font substitutions map automatically: `Newsreader` transitions to high-contrast Naskh/Amiri styling, and `Inter` transitions to `Tajawal`.
- Arabic line-heights are automatically increased by 15% relative to Latin sizes to accommodate full ascenders and descenders without clipping diacritics.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is constructed around an asymmetric **12-column institutional grid**, anchored by an executive 280px left dossier sidebar on desktop screens and a primary content canvas framed by structured safe margins.

- **Desktop Layout (≥1280px):** 12 columns with a 24px (`1.5rem`) gutter and 48px (`3rem`) page margins. Key evidence dossiers span 8 columns, flanked by a 4-column statutory metadata inspector.
- **Tablet Layout (768px - 1279px):** 8 columns with a 16px gutter; the secondary inspector folds into slide-over panels or drawers.
- **Mobile Layout (<768px):** 4 columns with 12px gutters and 16px margins. Complex multi-column legal comparisons stack vertically with strict chronological ordering preserved.

### Print A4 Formatting Rules
When triggered for physical court filing or executive export (`@media print`):
- All canvases enforce an exact ISO A4 ratio (`210mm x 297mm`) with `18mm` margins.
- Interactivity elements, accordions, and buttons strip out their control chrome. Accordions automatically expand to display all nested evidentiary disclosures.
- Colors switch to monochrome-optimized levels: navy elements shift to pure `#000000`, while amber borders shift to crisp 1pt `#475569` hairline dividers.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids floating, hyper-blurred, or casual neo-morphic shadows. Visual depth is established via **architectural layering and low-contrast borders**:

- **Ground Level (Base Canvas):** `#f8fafc` serves as the neutral legal ground.
- **Level 1 (Dossier & Record Cards):** Crisp `#ffffff` surfaces bordered by a hairline 1px border of `#e2e8f0`. Shadow is an ultra-subtle directional wash: `0 1px 3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05)`.
- **Level 2 (Active Legal Accordions & Hover States):** Surfaces lift subtly with an enriched border (`#cbd5e1`) and an ambient anchor shadow: `0 4px 12px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08)`.
- **Level 3 (Modal Lightbox & Evidence Viewers):** Deep royal backdrop overlay at 85% opacity (`rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.85)`) with backdrop filter blur (`blur(4px)`). The focal document window rises with an authoritative drop shadow: `0 20px 40px -8px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.35)`.
- **Golden Accents:** Amber accents (`#c59b27`) never cast colored glows. They act purely as structural boundaries (such as a 2px left-border on active exhibits) to command visual authority.

## Shapes

The geometric vocabulary prioritizes **sharp, disciplined rectilinearity** consistent with legal certificates, ledger entries, and formal filings.

- A `roundedness` level of `1` (Soft) is enforced universally.
- Base cards, form inputs, and buttons utilize a crisp `0.25rem` (4px) corner radius.
- Badges and status lozenges utilize `0.25rem` (4px) or completely straight square geometry (`0px`) for stamp-like formality. Circular or pill shapes (`9999px`) are forbidden across all interfaces to prevent visual flippancy.
- Document Lightbox containers and evidence modals max out at `rounded-lg` (`0.5rem` / 8px).

## Components

### 1. Executive Badges & Status Seals
- **Structure:** Monospaced label typography, uppercase tracking (`label-sm`), with a tight `0.25rem 0.5rem` padding.
- **States:**
  - *Verified / Sealed:* Emerald background tint (`#ecfdf5`), border `#10b981`, text `#065f46`.
  - *Under Arbitration / In Review:* Amber background tint (`#fffbeb`), border `#c59b27`, text `#92400e`.
  - *Statutory Notice:* Royal Navy background (`#0f172a`), border `#1e293b`, text `#f8fafc`.

### 2. Interactive Legal Source Accordions
- **Header:** Clean `#ffffff` bar bordered with 1px `#e2e8f0`, containing: Exhibit Number, Title, Judicial Date, and an indicator icon that rotates 90 degrees upon expansion.
- **Expanded Panel:** Inset background of `#f1f5f9` with a solid 3px left border colored in `#c59b27` (Golden Amber). Text within is formatted in `body-md` with citation blocks highlighted in high-contrast navy italicized quotes.

### 3. Structured Evidence Tabs
- **Layout:** Horizontal segment bar on desktop; scrollable segment bar on mobile.
- **Default State:** Transparent background, muted text (`#64748b`), no border.
- **Active State:** Text shifts to `#0f172a`, supported by an immediate bottom indicator bar (2px solid `#c59b27`) and a subtle paper-white card highlight.

### 4. Modal Lightbox (Document & Exhibit Viewer)
- **Container:** Full-screen modal utilizing the Level 3 overlay. Document viewport provides zoom/pan controls, page navigation counters (`1 / 24`), a watermark indicator displaying company registration credentials, and direct PDF download/print triggers.
- **Header:** Houses case reference metadata, confidentiality level classification, and bilingual language toggle.

### 5. Buttons & Actions
- **Primary:** Background `#0f172a`, text `#ffffff`, border 1px solid `#0f172a`. Hover transitions to `#1e293b` with a hairline secondary gold bottom border.
- **Secondary (Executive Gold):** Background `#c59b27`, text `#ffffff`, hover `#b4881f`.
- **Ghost / Docket Outline:** Background transparent, text `#0f172a`, border 1px solid `#cbd5e1`. Hover fills to `#f8fafc`.

### 6. Form Inputs & Search Filters
- **Input Fields:** Crisp `#ffffff` fill, 1px solid `#cbd5e1` border, 8px padding, focus states highlight with a 1.5px `#0f172a` ring and `#c59b27` caret.
